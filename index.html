About this tool

Plan your monthly budget across income, housing, groceries, fixed costs, and savings goals. The Budget Planner guides you through a step-by-step wizard, then shows a clear dashboard of where your money goes and how much you can save.

Ideal for Australians setting up their first budget, reviewing their spending after a pay rise, or planning how to reach a savings goal faster. No sign-up required — all data stays in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Budget Planner store my data?

No. All data is saved to your browser's local storage and never sent to any server. Clearing your browser storage will reset the planner.

What is the savings rate and why does it matter?

Your savings rate is the percentage of your take-home income you save or invest each month. A higher savings rate is the single biggest lever for reaching financial independence — even more than investment returns.

Can I upload my payslip?

Yes. The income step lets you upload an Australian payslip PDF to automatically parse your gross income, tax, and super — so you don't have to enter them manually.

How is tax calculated?

The planner estimates Australian income tax using the current ATO tax brackets and the low-income tax offset (LITO). The estimate is a guide — your actual tax will depend on deductions, Medicare levy, and other personal circumstances.
